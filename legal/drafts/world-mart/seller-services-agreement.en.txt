## 1 Appointment and purpose

Seller appoints World, on a non-exclusive basis, to host Seller's approved listings, to receive and administer orders, and to perform the services expressly included in the schedules to this agreement. Seller is the supplier of the goods and the exporter of record. Seller keeps ownership of its products until ownership passes to the buyer under the sale contract, and Seller bears all manufacturing, product and export responsibilities. World does not become a buyer, reseller, manufacturer or importer merely because it administers transactions. Any different arrangement requires a written amendment signed by both parties after legal review.

Seller authorises World to act as its disclosed and limited agent to accept orders only within the limits set out in Schedule B and to arrange identified third-party services. World has no authority to change Seller's prices, to grant credit, to settle litigation, to make regulatory declarations outside a separate broker mandate, or to bind Seller beyond those limits. Documents issued to buyers disclose both the actual seller of the goods and World as the service provider.

## 2 Conditions precedent

Seller has no trading access until all of the following have been verified and passed: its legal status; its Indian manufacturing or brand rights; an annual turnover of at least INR 30 crore; World's internal verification; independent certification; clearance of each product for each destination country; insurance; and signed payment mandates. World records the approved facilities, products, countries and sales channels in Schedule A. These conditions must continue to be met throughout the term. If any requirement expires or is suspended, new orders and scheduled purchases affected by it are blocked until it is restored.

## 3 Services and standards

Schedule B specifies, for each service, whether World performs the task itself or appoints a provider, together with the deliverable, the standard, the dependencies, the responsible party and the fee. The services available include supplier assessment, sourcing, inspection, order administration, warehousing, packaging, shipping, customs coordination, installation and after-sales administration. World exercises reasonable skill and care in the services it undertakes. An inspection is limited to its agreed sampling method and scope. It does not guarantee every unit and does not relieve Seller of its obligation to supply conforming goods.

Seller provides accurate specifications and documents, access to its sites, information on stock availability and manufacturing capacity, and trained contact persons. World selects competent logistics and technical partners and gives them instructions within its authority. World keeps the liability and insurance of those providers in place through its partner contracts, and World cannot avoid its own obligations simply by subcontracting them. No operational service begins until the lawful local permissions it needs are in place.

## 4 Fees invoices and certification funding

Schedule C and the Seller Hub fee schedule contain the binding commercial arrangement. Commission is charged on the net value of accepted goods after any discounts funded by Seller, and excludes separately itemised taxes, duties, freight, insurance and buyer service fees. The commission percentage for each product category, and for the B2B and B2C tiers, is set out in the Seller Hub fee schedule and is shown to Seller before trading begins. Any optional subscription, onboarding or service fee identifies what Seller receives for it and how a refund of it is treated.

World appoints and pays independent certification bodies. Any recovery of certification costs funded by buyers is disclosed to the buyer before the order is placed and is recorded in the ledger against the relevant certification programme. It is never imposed on a buyer after the order. Seller pays for retesting, re-audit or remediation that relates specifically to Seller only where the fault-based schedule in Schedule C makes Seller responsible. No fee buys approval. Where an order is cancelled because of the supplier's fault, the commission on it is reversed and any service amounts not yet earned are returned in accordance with Schedule C.

Invoices show platform service fees, taxes and pass-through expenses as separate items. The method of currency conversion and the allocation of payment processing costs are set out in the Seller Hub fee schedule before any order is accepted. A dispute about an invoice must identify the specific lines disputed, and undisputed amounts remain payable. Freight and certification costs are never charged twice, and no charge is made that has not been disclosed to Seller in advance.

## 5 Escrow settlement and security

Payments are collected and paid out through World's authorised payment partners: Stripe Payments Europe Ltd and Stripe, Inc. (cards, international payments and seller payouts through Stripe Connect) and Razorpay Software Private Limited (India). Funds are held and settled in the bank and account structure operated by those providers under their own authorisations. Seller accepts the onboarding and settlement terms of the relevant provider, which Seller agrees to separately when it connects its payout account in the Seller Hub. World may send allocation, refund and hold instructions to a provider only as that provider's terms permit. This agreement does not create any unlicensed payment service and gives World no right to appropriate funds held for goods.

Schedule D records the payout milestones, the deductions permitted, the reserve percentage, its cap and duration, the handling of disputes and the release of funds. Settlement deadlines required by law or by the provider override any commercial hold. Where proceeds cannot lawfully be held for a long period, security may be provided by a separate guarantee or other approved collateral. World gives reasons for a risk hold where it is lawful to do so, reviews each hold at least once a month, and promptly releases any amount that exceeds the exposure it covers. Deposits and guarantees are refunded or discharged once the exposures they cover have ended, subject to any claims supported by evidence.

## 6 Product compliance warranties and territory

Seller warrants that its goods are authentic; are new unless otherwise specifically agreed; are fit for the intended use that has been expressly accepted; conform to their specifications; are free from manufacturing defects; and may lawfully be marketed in each approved country. Seller holds every required licence, technical file, label, declaration and brand right, and maintains traceability. Seller performs every recall, incident-reporting and regulator-cooperation duty that the law assigns to it. World's approval of a product does not authorise its sale in a country that has not been approved.

For OEM orders, Schedule E allocates designs, tooling, intellectual property, reference units, acceptance tests, manufacturing approvals and confidentiality. Seller must not change the factory, materials, any critical process, the formulation, the design or any safety software without prior approval and revalidation. Seller controls the claims and instructions that accompany its goods downstream and must not make any representation about certification that it cannot support.

## 7 Transport custody title and import responsibilities

The default term of sale for international B2B orders is DAP (named place of destination) under Incoterms 2020, with Seller as exporter and the buyer as importer. Seller bears the transport risk until the goods are made available at the named DAP place, ready for unloading, regardless of which party books the carriage. The buyer pays import duties, taxes, clearance and unloading unless the accepted order lawfully provides otherwise. World's logistics and coordination charges are paid by Seller under Schedule C and may be included in the delivery price disclosed to the buyer. Reconciliation ensures that the same cost is never recovered twice.

For each leg of custody, the records identify the goods, the count, the seal, the condition, the temperature where relevant, and the party bearing the risk. Cargo insurance is arranged separately where appropriate. Liability of World or a warehouse for a breach of custody follows the agreed scope of services, the law and the insurance in place, and Seller remains responsible for defects that existed before custody began. The passing of ownership is stated separately in the goods order and is not determined by Incoterms. For B2C sales, risk and import disclosures follow mandatory consumer law and the World consumer terms.

## 8 Complaints recalls and insurance

Seller responds to ordinary complaints within seventy-two hours, reports serious safety events immediately and within any deadline set by applicable law, and follows the recall procedure. Seller pays for reasonable remedies relating to the goods and for investigation costs to the extent they are attributable to Seller. World administers cases and payment instructions and does not prevent any party from approaching a regulator or a court. Any recovery in a settlement is limited to amounts actually incurred and supported by evidence, and to lawful set-off.

Schedule F sets out the required policy limits, territories, exclusions, deductibles, additional-insured endorsements and expiry dates. Product liability insurance and recall insurance are mandatory for every product within the approved scope. A refusal by an insurer to pay does not remove any of Seller's obligations. Seller provides evidence of renewal before each policy expires and promptly reports any reduction or cancellation of cover.

## 9 Confidentiality data and intellectual property

Each party protects the other's confidential commercial, technical, customer and assessment information with reasonable controls. This obligation does not apply to information that is lawfully public, that a party developed independently, or that it received without restriction. Where a party is legally compelled to disclose confidential information, it notifies the other party first where it is lawful to do so. Confidentiality obligations survive for five years after termination, and trade secrets remain protected for as long as they remain confidential.

Seller keeps ownership of the listing content it supplies and grants World a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence to host, store, reproduce, display, resize, translate and format that content in order to operate and promote the marketplace and for approved marketing. The licence lasts for as long as the content is on the Platform, plus a reasonable period for backups and legal records. It does not permit World to use the content to train general-purpose AI models or in third-party advertising without Seller's separate consent. Seller warrants that it holds the rights to the content it supplies and reviews any translated safety claims. World is an independent controller of platform data. Seller is an independent controller of the buyer data it receives to fulfil an order, and must use that data only for that order; Seller must not sell buyer data or use it for unrelated marketing. Both parties apply any processor and international-transfer terms required by law. Any AI-assisted assessment must be based on source evidence, and decisions are taken by people. Personal data is retained only for as long as its legal purpose requires, rather than under a general commercial archive period.

## 10 Indemnity and limitations

Seller indemnifies World against third-party claims, and against reasonable documented losses, caused by goods supplied by Seller that are defective, unsafe, counterfeit, infringing or unlawful, by inaccurate declarations, by a breach of export rules, by a recall, or by any other breach by Seller. This indemnity does not cover any part of a loss caused by World's own breach, negligence, fraud or wilful misconduct. World gives Seller prompt notice of a claim and reasonable control of its defence, and cooperates in it. Seller must not settle any claim in a way that requires an admission by World, imposes a non-monetary obligation on World or leaves World exposed to unprotected liability, without World's consent.

World's total liability for ordinary contractual claims relating to its services is limited to the total fees paid by Seller to World in the twelve months before the event giving rise to the claim. Pass-through costs, taxes and funds for goods are not fees for this purpose. To the extent the law allows, neither party is liable for indirect losses, but this exclusion does not apply to third-party claims covered by an indemnity. The cap and the exclusions do not limit liability for fraud, wilful misconduct, gross negligence, death or personal injury caused by negligence, the safeguarding or return of funds, statutory duties that cannot be excluded, breaches of confidentiality, data protection or intellectual property, or any other liability that cannot be limited by law. Seller's warranty, safety, recall and indemnity obligations for its goods are not subject to the cap.

## 11 Term termination and unresolved orders

The initial term is one year from the date this agreement takes effect. It may be renewed by agreement or by a renewal acceptance recorded on the Platform. Renewal requires every eligibility condition and all scope evidence to remain valid. Either party may terminate this agreement for convenience on thirty days' written notice. A party in material breach has fifteen days from notice to remedy it. Illegality, a critical safety issue, fraud or the loss of a mandatory authorisation entitles World to suspend or terminate this agreement immediately.

On termination, no new orders are accepted, but accepted lawful orders, refunds, complaints, warranties, recalls, evidence and accrued payment obligations continue to be honoured. World produces a closing statement and releases collateral and undisputed amounts in accordance with the provider's rules and the exposure that remains covered. Data and content are removed or retained in accordance with the law and the agreed purposes. Security is never forfeited automatically.

## 12 Disputes notices and precedence

This agreement is governed by the laws of India, subject to any mandatory obligations of the destination country. Subject to the arbitration agreement below, the courts at New Delhi, India have exclusive jurisdiction. The parties first escalate any dispute to their authorised representatives for fifteen days of good-faith discussion. If the dispute is not resolved, it is referred to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 before a sole arbitrator appointed by mutual agreement, or through the statutory appointment process if the parties cannot agree. Neither party may appoint the sole arbitrator alone. The seat and venue of arbitration is New Delhi, India, and the language is English. The courts may still grant lawful interim relief and hear matters that cannot be arbitrated.

Notices to Seller are sent to the email address and registered office recorded in Seller's verified Seller Hub account. Notices to World are sent to legal@worldmart.com and by post to its registered office. This agreement may be amended only in writing, either signed or accepted electronically as a new version on the Platform. A party relying on force majeure must give notice, take reasonable steps to mitigate, and deal with delayed orders lawfully; force majeure does not excuse any refund that is due. Seller may not assign this agreement without World's consent. World may assign it to a successor that takes over its obligations. Nothing in this agreement creates employment, a partnership, a general agency or a joint venture.

Mandatory law prevails. This agreement and its schedules govern the services World provides to Seller. The World seller terms supplement Seller's account obligations, and confirmed orders govern the specific details of the goods. Seller's own terms for its goods do not bind World unless World expressly accepts them, and they cannot limit consumer rights. No update to the website overrides a schedule that has been individually negotiated and accepted, unless Seller accepts the change.
