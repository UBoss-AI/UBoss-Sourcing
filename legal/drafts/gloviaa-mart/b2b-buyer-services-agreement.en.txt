## 1 Purpose and transaction structure

Gloviaa provides a managed purchasing interface and the services specified below. Buyer may purchase approved goods through one checkout, but each confirmed line identifies its actual Indian seller. Unless a separately signed reviewed contract provides otherwise, goods are sold by Seller to Buyer; Gloviaa supplies platform and managed services. Buyer authorises Gloviaa as disclosed limited agent for order coordination and broker or logistics arrangements within the agreed mandate, not for unlimited commitments or regulatory declarations in Buyer's name.

The goods contract specifies product requirements seller price delivery title warranty and remedies. Gloviaa's services agreement governs its own deliverables and fees. No label removes duties arising from Gloviaa's actual activities or applicable law.

## 7 Export import transport and ownership

Seller is exporter of record and Buyer importer of record for the international B2B launch. Buyer maintains customs and product licences and appoints a lawful broker; Gloviaa coordinates documents and partners through limited written authority. Product-specific responsible-person or importer obligations cannot be assigned to an incapable purchaser. Do not dispatch until a compliant route is confirmed.

Default goods delivery is DAP named destination Incoterms 2020. Seller bears transit risk to the agreed place ready for unloading. Buyer bears import clearance duties taxes and unloading. An order may use FCA with Buyer bearing main-carriage risk only where expressly agreed. DDP is unavailable without a reviewed lawful importer and tax arrangement. Title transfers at the expressly stated goods-contract point, separate from transit risk. Goods invoicing and customs descriptions must match the true transaction.
