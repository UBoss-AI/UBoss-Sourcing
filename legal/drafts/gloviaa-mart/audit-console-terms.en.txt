## Part C - Terms for Audit Console users

## C1. Your access

Access to the Audit Console is by invitation only. Your role decides what you can see and do: an inspector sees only the jobs assigned to them, and an agency's roster and invoices are shown only to the roles that need them. You must use the console only for the inspections, reviews and compliance work assigned to you or your agency, and only within your role. You must set up and use two-step sign-in, and you must not share your account.

## C2. Independence and conflicts of interest

You must carry out inspections and reviews independently and impartially. You must not accept a job, or must withdraw from it, if you or your agency have an interest in the seller, the buyer or the goods, or any other relationship that could affect, or appear to affect, your judgement, and you must tell the marketplace about it. You must not accept any gift, payment or favour from a buyer or a seller.

## C3. No contact outside the console

You must communicate with buyers and sellers about a job only through the means the console and the inspection process provide. You must not use contact details you see to approach a buyer or a seller for any other purpose, including to offer your own services.

## C4. Evidence and reports

Evidence you upload - photographs, measurements, documents - must be genuine, unaltered, captured at the inspection it belongs to, and recorded with its true capture time. The same file must not be used for more than one inspection unless it truly belongs to each. Reports must state what you actually found and checked, including any limitation of the inspection, and must not state that a document is authentic unless you verified it by a method the process allows; record the method you used. A submitted report is kept as a revision; corrections are made as a new revision, never by changing the record of what was submitted.

When you review compliance documents or rules, record your decision and reasons truthfully, and follow the process - including the requirement that a rule be approved by a second person.

## C5. Confidentiality and personal data

Everything you see in the Audit Console - orders, products, sellers' documents, buyers' details, evidence and other agencies' work - is confidential and may be used only for the assigned work. You must handle personal data in line with the Privacy Policy and the data-protection law that applies; avoid capturing people's faces or other personal data in photographs unless the inspection requires it; not copy evidence or documents out of the console except as the work requires; and report any loss or misuse of data without delay. These duties continue after your access ends.

## C6. Invoices

Invoices your agency submits through the console must be accurate and relate only to work actually done. Payment of an agency's invoice is made outside the Platform under the agency's agreement.

## C7. Ending access

Your agency, or Gloviaa Mart, may remove your access at any time. Your obligations under sections C2 to C5 continue after your access ends.
