# Runway Handoff Package

> **Status: NOT READY — DRAFT FOR APPROVAL.** This package states what the
> Runway production phase must respect. It deliberately contains **no generation
> prompts**; those are written only after the owner explicitly approves this
> package and the Definition of Ready in
> [VIDEO_REQUIREMENTS.md](VIDEO_REQUIREMENTS.md) §19 is met.
> No Runway credits have been used.

## 1. Exact brand identity

```
Gloviaa Mart
Source with Intelligence | Deliver with Confidence
Powered by UBOSS
```

- "Gloviaa Mart" — double "a". "UBOSS" — all capitals.
- The tagline uses a vertical bar with one space either side.
- **Never** use "The Way to the World", "The Way to the Global Sourcing",
  "Glovia", "UBoss" or "Gloviaa Market".
- All text (logo, tagline, captions, title cards) is set in the edit, never
  generated by Runway.

## 6. Verified features (may be shown)

Shown only as **real screen recordings**, never generated:

- Homepage, catalogue, search, product page with variants
- Language selection (eight languages) and currency (PLN, EUR)
- Individual and company sign-in tabs
- Preorder rules and form (minimum quantity, "a request is not an order")
- Preorder chat with suggested questions and the human-agent button
- Cart and checkout up to payment
- Order confirmation and order history
- Logistics portal screens: shipments, legs, drivers (phase 2)

Shown only once their blocker is cleared: container ordering (B-03), Stripe test
payment (B-05), L1–L4 tracking on an order (B-03), every Seller Hub chapter
(B-04).

## 8. Approved claims

- Every seller is reviewed before they can sell.
- Businesses buy through a company account that the team checks first.
- See prices in your own currency and language.
- Buy by the piece, by the carton, or ask a seller to make a container load.
- Order from several sellers in one checkout.
- Every delivery is planned leg by leg, from the factory to your door.
- An order is confirmed only when the payment provider confirms payment.
- Designed for buyers in Europe, beginning with Poland.
- Built in India.
- *(After B-10 only)* Independent inspection before dispatch, where the
  marketplace's rules require it.

## 9. Prohibited claims

Guaranteed quality; guaranteed or on-time delivery; real-time global tracking;
fully verified sellers or products; secure / bank-grade payments; available
worldwide or "now live"; best or lowest prices; largest network; fully automated
logistics; complete regulatory compliance or certification; AI that finds
anything; real-time translation; any unapproved tagline. Full table:
[ABOUT_US_VIDEO_BRIEF.md](ABOUT_US_VIDEO_BRIEF.md) §5.

## 10. Presenter specification (PROVISIONAL — D-08)

- One consistent, **original** adult presenter; must not resemble any
  celebrity, public figure or real person
- Approximately 30–40, professional South Asian appearance, gender per D-08a
  (default female)
- Business casual: plain navy or charcoal blazer, light shirt, no logos
- Premium modern studio, soft neutral background with a subtle navy-to-blue
  gradient
- Waist-up framing, natural eye contact, soft professional lighting
- Warm, calm, trustworthy; restrained open-hand gestures; never pointing
- Full screen for introduction, chapter transitions and conclusion;
  picture-in-picture only where it covers no interface element
- One reference image approved once and reused for every generation

## 11. Voice specification (PROVISIONAL — D-08, D-13)

- Original synthetic voice; **no cloning** of any person without their written
  consent
- Clear international English; light Indian English warmth acceptable
- 140–150 wpm in the demo; 120–135 wpm in the About Us film
- Warm, confident, never hype
- Pronunciation: Gloviaa = glow-VEE-ah; UBOSS = YOO-boss (to be confirmed)
- The same voice narrates the About Us film and speaks for the presenter

## 18. Quality requirements

- 1920×1080 minimum delivery; 4K upscale of the locked About Us cut only
- No visible generation artefacts: warped hands, melting objects, flicker,
  identity drift between presenter takes
- Lip-sync within one frame of the final narration
- Loudness: −14 LUFS integrated for online platforms, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP
- Captions accurate, human-checked, synced within 100 ms
- Colour consistent with the brand palette across every scene

## 19. Safety requirements

- No real person's face, voice or likeness without written consent
- No impersonation of a celebrity, public figure, company or competitor
- No real third-party brands or trademarked designs
- No real customer, seller or staff data on screen
- No passwords, keys, tokens, real card or bank details
- No claim from the prohibited list
- A "fictional data, test mode" disclaimer on the demo videos (L-01)
- AI-generated content labelled where the publishing platform requires it
