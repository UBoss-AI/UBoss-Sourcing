# About Us Video — Creative Brief

> **Status: DRAFT FOR APPROVAL.** All choices marked PROVISIONAL follow the
> recommended options in [VIDEO_REQUIREMENTS.md](VIDEO_REQUIREMENTS.md) §15.
> This is a brief, not a Runway prompt. No video has been generated.

## 1. Objective

**Primary:** make a European company buyer — a procurement manager in Poland is
the reference viewer — believe that Gloviaa Mart is a careful, transparent and
capable way to source from Indian sellers.

**Secondary:**

1. Show Indian manufacturers and export-focused sellers that this is a serious
   route to buyers abroad.
2. Explain, in one picture, that buyers, sellers, logistics partners and
   inspection come together on one order.

## 2. Audience

| Priority | Viewer | What they must feel at the end |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Company buyers, procurement teams (Europe, Poland first) | "These people are careful. I would trust them with a first order." |
| 2 | Indian sellers and manufacturers | "This could take my products to Europe." |
| 3 | Partners and investors (re-use) | "This is a real, coherent system." |

## 3. Core message

### Options considered

| # | Core message | Fit |
|---|---|---|
| A | **Sourcing from India, made clear and careful.** One connected place where buyers find sellers, see the terms up front, and follow every leg of delivery. | **Recommended** — combines trust and transparency, true to what is built |
| B | Connecting Indian sellers with global buyers | Strong for sellers; weaker for the primary buyer audience |
| C | Intelligent product sourcing | Leans on the AI Assistant, which is blocked today (B-07) |
| D | Transparent business purchasing | Good, but narrower than the ecosystem |
| E | Inspection and trust | Inspection gate evidence conflicts (B-10); cannot lead |
| F | One connected commerce ecosystem | Good as the visual idea; abstract as a message |

**Provisional choice: A**, with F as the visual device (the globe connecting
the groups). The tagline carries "intelligence" and "confidence" so the film
does not need to over-explain AI.

## 6. Creative direction

- **Cinematic 70 / corporate 30.** Feels like a premium brand film. The product
  appears, but as proof rather than a tour.
- **Human first.** People making, checking, packing and receiving goods. Hands,
  faces in soft focus, real-feeling work — never stock-photo smiles to camera.
- **One visual thread:** the rotating Gloviaa globe. It opens the film, lights
  up the route from India to Poland, and resolves into the logo lockup.
- **Honest and calm.** No countdowns, no "revolutionary", no fireworks.
- **No identifiable brands, logos, people or places** in generated scenes.
  Factories, ports and trucks are generic.

## 7. Visual style

| Element | Direction |
|---|---|
| Palette | Navy `#0C2350` and brand blue `#1D4ED8` as the base; warm action orange `#EA580C` used sparingly for moments of human warmth; teal `#0F766E` for logistics accents |
| Light | Early-morning and golden-hour light; soft, natural |
| Camera | Slow push-ins, gentle parallax, one or two smooth aerial moves; no shaky handheld |
| Type | Inter for on-screen text; the "Gloviaa Mart" wordmark in its script face (Dancing Script Bold) only in the logo lockup |
| Motion graphics | Thin glowing route lines on the globe; L1–L4 shown as four segments of one line |
| App inserts | Two or three, 2–3 s each, framed in a clean device or floating window — only after B-01/B-02 |
| Texture | Clean, premium, slightly cool; no grain or film burn |

## 10. Narration direction

- Same original synthetic voice as the demo presenter (see
  [VIDEO_REQUIREMENTS.md](VIDEO_REQUIREMENTS.md) §10).
- 120–135 words per minute, with space. About 110–130 words in the master.
- Warm, calm, sure. Sentences of 6–14 words.
- Say "Gloviaa" as **glow-VEE-ah** (pending D-13).
- The tagline is spoken once, slowly, near the end; on screen it is written
  exactly as `Source with Intelligence | Deliver with Confidence`.

## 11. Music direction

- Original or properly licensed (commercial, worldwide, online and paid media,
  perpetual). The licence document is filed in the asset register.
- Modern orchestral-ambient: soft piano and strings, a light pulse that builds
  under beats 4–5, resolves warmly at the lockup.
- 80–95 BPM. No vocals. No recognisable melody.
- A separate music stem for the muted loop and the social cuts.

## 15. Runway production recommendations

- Generate **scene by scene** (5–10 s clips), never the whole film in one go.
  Keep a fixed style reference frame for colour and light, and reuse it in
  every generation.
- Generate the globe and route animation as a separate layer if possible; the
  real globe from the product can also be screen-recorded as a plate (A-10).
- Build the logo lockup in an editor, not in Runway — generated text is
  unreliable. The same goes for every caption and title card.
- Generated people must be original and non-identifiable. Avoid close-ups of
  hands holding text, labels or packaging with writing; generated lettering
  comes out garbled.
- No real brand names, flags on uniforms, port names or vehicle liveries in any
  generated scene.
- Upscale only the final, locked cut to 4K.
- Keep a log of every prompt, seed and model version used, for later re-cuts.
